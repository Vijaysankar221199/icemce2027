Call for Papers

Share your research in intelligent energy systems, electronics and control

Scope of ICIESEC–2027

Original research contributions within the conference scope are invited from academicians, research scholars, scientists, engineers, industry professionals and students.

  • Intelligent Energy Systems, Energy Storage and Management, and Energy-Efficient Systems
  • Renewable Energy, Smart Grids, Microgrids and Power Quality
  • Power Electronics and Electrical Drives
  • Electric Vehicles and Sustainable Transportation
  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Smart Electronics and Embedded Systems
  • Intelligent Control and Automation
  • IoT and Cyber-Physical Systems
  • Robotics and Autonomous Systems
  • Sustainable and Emerging Engineering Technologies

The listed tracks are indicative. Related research within the scope of ICIESEC–2027 is also welcome.

Indicative Areas of Interest

  • Energy systems: renewable energy, smart grids, microgrids, power systems and energy storage
  • Electronics and intelligent systems: smart electronics, embedded systems, IoT and edge computing
  • Control and automation: intelligent control, predictive control, optimization and robotics
  • Electric vehicles and mobility: EV powertrains, battery management and charging infrastructure
  • Emerging technologies: AI applications in energy and control, Industry 4.0 and 5.0
