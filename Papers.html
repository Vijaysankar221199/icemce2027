Scope of ICIESEC–2027

ICIESEC–2027 provides a multidisciplinary forum for researchers and professionals working in intelligent energy, electronics, artificial intelligence and control.

  • Renewable Energy Technologies
  • Smart Grid Technologies and Microgrids
  • Power Electronics and Electrical Drives
  • Electric Vehicles and Sustainable Transportation
  • Smart Electronics and Embedded Systems
  • AI, ML, IoT and Cyber-Physical Systems
  • Robotics and Autonomous Systems
  • Energy Storage and Management
