Submission Guidelines

  1. Authors are invited to submit original research papers within the scope of ICIESEC–2027.
  2. Manuscripts must follow the prescribed IEEE manuscript format.
  3. Accepted papers must have at least one registered author for presentation.
  4. Camera-ready papers and copyright forms must be submitted within the specified deadline.
  5. Accepted papers will be considered for inclusion in IEEE Xplore, subject to final approval.
