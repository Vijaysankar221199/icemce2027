Paper Submission

Submit original research papers for ICIESEC 2027

Author Guidelines

  1. ICIESEC–2027 invites original, full-length research papers and review articles.
  2. The manuscript should be 6–8 pages in the prescribed IEEE conference paper format.
  3. Authors should explain the contribution, methodology or analysis, findings and conclusions clearly.
  4. Include all authors’ names, affiliations and email addresses, and identify the corresponding author.
  5. Submit work that has not been published previously and is not under review elsewhere.
  6. Submit through the conference’s online paper submission portal.
  7. All submissions will undergo peer review. The review decision and requested revisions will be communicated to the corresponding author.
  8. At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present it at the conference.
  9. Authors must submit the final camera-ready manuscript and required copyright form by the announced deadline.

Submission Information

  • Submission platform: will be supplied
  • Paper template: https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates
  • IEEE conference code: To be confirmed
  • Mode: Hybrid
