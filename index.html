About the Conference

Advancing intelligent energy systems, electronics and control

ICIESEC–2027 provides an international forum for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, research scholars and students to present their work, exchange knowledge and discuss emerging research challenges in intelligent energy systems, electronics and control.

The conference covers green energy technologies, smart grids, energy storage, power electronics, electrical drives, electric vehicles, smart electronics, artificial intelligence, intelligent control, automation and sustainable engineering.

Key Focus Areas

  • Intelligent Energy Systems, Energy Storage and Management
  • Renewable Energy, Smart Grids, Microgrids and Power Quality
  • Power Electronics and Electrical Drives
  • Electric Vehicles, AI, IoT, Robotics and Sustainable Engineering
