About the Conference

Driving innovation across energy, materials, and communication

Welcome to the official website of ICEMCE 2027. This conference is designed to bring together scholars, policymakers, researchers, and industry professionals to exchange ideas, foster collaboration, and showcase transformative research.

The conference will focus on emerging trends in intelligent energy systems, resilient communication networks, functional materials, sustainable manufacturing, and interdisciplinary engineering approaches.
